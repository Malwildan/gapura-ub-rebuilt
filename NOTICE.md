# Attribution and provenance

This is an independent, unofficial reconstruction of Gapura UB 3.3.38. It is not an official Universitas Brawijaya release or an assertion of endorsement.

Names, logos, artwork, bundled fonts and institutional text retained from the target application remain attributable to their respective owners. Their inclusion here does not grant additional rights or place them under an invented project-wide license. No original APK, native application library, publisher signing key or private account data is included.

`assets/provenance.json` records 106 asset hashes and their historical locations in the analyzed target artifact. Those paths are provenance references, not runtime dependencies. The `source_apk_sha256` identifies the verified base APK used during reconstruction. The public-content fixtures under `test/fixtures` retain only expected institutional content used by the source tests. The screenshot in `docs/images` shows the rebuilt Menu without personal account details.

Flutter, Gradle and the dependencies listed in `pubspec.lock` retain their own licenses. The included standard Gradle wrapper is build tooling, not a rebuilt application binary.
